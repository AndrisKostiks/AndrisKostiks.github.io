# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-05-27**

## Overview

This is a personal finance application used exclusively by its owner (Andris Kostiks) to aggregate and review personal bank account and transaction data. It is not a commercial product and has no end users other than its owner.

## Data collected

The application connects to bank accounts via the Enable Banking PSD2 AISP interface in **read-only** mode. It collects:

- Account identifiers and balances
- Transaction history (amounts, dates, counterparty names, descriptions)
- OAuth session tokens required to maintain bank connectivity

## How data is stored

All data is stored locally on the owner's personal hardware. It is never transmitted to any third party, cloud service, or remote server. The application runs exclusively on a home network and is not accessible from the public internet.

## Data retention

Data is retained indefinitely for personal record-keeping purposes and may be deleted by the owner at any time.

## Third-party access

No third parties have access to any data collected by this application. Bank connectivity is established solely through Enable Banking's regulated AISP interface.
